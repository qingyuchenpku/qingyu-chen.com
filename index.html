Qingyu Chen 陈庆雨

DPhil Candidate in Economics, University of Oxford

I will be visiting the Department of Economics at Yale University in Spring 2027.

Hello! I am a DPhil (PhD) candidate in Economics at the University of Oxford and Nuffield College. I am also affiliated with the Centre for the Study of African Economies (CSAE).

My research lies in international trade and development economics, with a particular focus on industrialization in the Global South. I study how trade and industrial policies, international market access, and foreign direct investment reshape economic activity, and what these changes imply for structural transformation and the allocation of resources across firms, industries, and regions.

I hold an MPhil in Economics (with distinction) also from the University of Oxford, and a BA in Finance from Peking University.
